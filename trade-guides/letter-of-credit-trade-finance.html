Eliminating Counterparty Risk in High-Value Trade

In multi-container and bulk export transactions, financial security is paramount. A Letter of Credit (LC) represents a binding commitment by an issuing bank on behalf of the buyer to pay the seller upon presentation of strictly complying shipping and inspection documents. Neither party has to rely on trust alone—the bank's credit replaces commercial counterparty risk.

Frequently Asked Questions & Commercial Guidance

Institutional buyer inquiries regarding specifications, maritime shipping, minimum order quantities, and payment terms.

What type of Letter of Credit does AmBright Global accept?
We accept Irrevocable Confirmed Letter of Credit payable at sight (LC at Sight) issued by tier-1 global banks (e.g., HSBC, Standard Chartered, BNP Paribas, Citi, Emirates NBD) and confirmed by prime Indian or international banking institutions.
How does escrow payment work for first-time buyers?
For buyers wishing to inspect goods before funds leave their control, we partner with licensed institutional trade escrow platforms. The buyer deposits the invoice funds into an escrow account held at a prime bank. The funds are only disbursed to AmBright Global after an independent surveyor (e.g. SGS) uploads the clean inspection certificate.
