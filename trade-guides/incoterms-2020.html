FOB, CIF, CFR, DAP & EXW Demystified

Incoterms (International Commercial Terms) are standard commercial contractual terms created by the International Chamber of Commerce (ICC) to define the exact point where costs and risks transfer between a seller and a buyer in cross-border trade. Selecting the correct Incoterm prevents costly misunderstandings over container terminal handling charges (THC), demurrage, customs clearance, and marine insurance claims.

Incoterms 2020 Responsibility & Risk Allocation Matrix

Incoterm Term Name Cost Responsibility Risk Transfer Point Marine Insurance Recommended Use
FOB Free on Board Seller pays to Indian port; Buyer pays ocean freight When cargo passes ship's rail at Indian POL Buyer arranges insurance Buyers with preferred carrier contracts
CIF Cost, Insurance & Freight Seller pays to destination port + insurance When cargo is loaded on vessel in India Seller provides Institute Cargo Clauses Most popular for B2B bulk buyers
CFR Cost and Freight Seller pays ocean freight to destination When cargo is loaded on board at POL Buyer arranges insurance Buyers with global open marine policies
DAP Delivered at Place Seller pays freight to buyer's warehouse At buyer's door prior to unloading Seller arranges full transit cover Turnkey institutional buyers
EXW Ex Works Buyer pays all freight from Indian factory At factory floor in India Buyer arranges full coverage Rare in ocean trade; high buyer friction

Frequently Asked Questions & Commercial Guidance

Institutional buyer inquiries regarding specifications, maritime shipping, minimum order quantities, and payment terms.

Under CIF, where does risk actually transfer?
Under CIF Incoterms 2020, risk transfers from seller to buyer the moment the goods are loaded on board the vessel at the port of origin (e.g. Nhava Sheva). However, the seller is legally obligated to contract and pay for ocean freight and marine insurance to the destination port.
What level of cargo insurance does AmBright Global provide under CIF?
Unlike minimal Institute Cargo Clauses (C) which only cover major casualties like vessel sinkings, AmBright Global provides comprehensive Institute Cargo Clauses (A) 'All Risks' coverage plus Institute War Clauses and Strikes Clauses, with coverage set at 110% of CIF value as standard practice.
