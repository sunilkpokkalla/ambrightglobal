Frequently Asked Questions & Commercial Guidance

Institutional buyer inquiries regarding specifications, maritime shipping, minimum order quantities, and payment terms.

Do you handle US Customs Importer Security Filing (ISF 10+2)?
Yes. We transmit all 10 required data elements (Manufacturer/Supplier, Seller, Buyer, Ship-to, Container Stuffing Location, Consolidator, Importer of Record #, Consignee #, Country of Origin, and HTSUS numbers) to your US customs broker at least 72 hours prior to vessel loading in India.
Is your facility registered with the US FDA?
Yes. AmBright Global Traders holds active US FDA Food Facility Registration and files Prior Notice for every food and agricultural shipment destined for the United States.
