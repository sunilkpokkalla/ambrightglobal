Frequently Asked Questions & Commercial Guidance

Institutional buyer inquiries regarding specifications, maritime shipping, minimum order quantities, and payment terms.

How do you guarantee EU pesticide MRL compliance for spices and grains?
Every production lot undergoes comprehensive 500+ multi-residue pesticide screening at NABL-accredited, ISO 17025 certified laboratories before shipping. Consignments with detectable banned substances are automatically rejected.
Can you provide REX (Registered Exporter System) declarations for duty concessions?
Yes. AmBright Global is registered in the European Commission's REX system, allowing us to generate Statement on Origin on commercial invoices for eligible preferential tariff treatment.
