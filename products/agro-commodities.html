Frequently Asked Questions & Commercial Guidance

Institutional buyer inquiries regarding specifications, maritime shipping, minimum order quantities, and payment terms.

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ) for spices and Basmati rice?
For black pepper, cardamom, and turmeric, our standard export MOQ is 1 × 20' Full Container Load (approx. 14 to 18 MT depending on packaging). For Basmati and non-basmati rice, MOQ is 1 × 20' FCL (approx. 25 MT). Mixed commodity container stuffing is available for approved distributors.
Do you provide Phytosanitary and Spices Board of India certificates?
Yes. Every export consignment is accompanied by an official government Phytosanitary Certificate issued by the Ministry of Agriculture, along with Spices Board / APEDA quality certificates and non-radiation declarations.
What payment terms are accepted for agricultural commodity exports?
We operate primarily on Irrevocable Confirmed Letter of Credit at sight (LC at Sight) issued by prime international banks, or 30% advance deposit with balance against scanned original Bill of Lading (BL) copy. Escrow payment through approved trade banks is also supported.
Can we specify private label retail packaging for rice and spices?
Yes. In addition to bulk 25 kg/50 kg PP bags, we provide custom retail packaging under your brand name (1 kg, 5 kg, 10 kg BOPP or zipper pouches with barcode, nutrition panel, and multilingual typography).
What are typical ocean shipping transit times from Indian ports?
Transit times depend on destination: Middle East (Jebel Ali / Dammam) takes 3–5 days; Southeast Asia (Singapore / Port Klang) takes 6–9 days; Europe (Rotterdam / Hamburg) takes 18–24 days; US East Coast takes 24–28 days.
