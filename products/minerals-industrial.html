Frequently Asked Questions & Commercial Guidance

Institutional buyer inquiries regarding specifications, maritime shipping, minimum order quantities, and payment terms.

Can you supply API 13A bentonite in break-bulk vessel charter?
Yes. For infrastructure and drilling projects requiring large volumes (3,000 MT to 25,000 MT), we arrange charter or part-cargo bulk vessels out of Kandla Port, in addition to standard containerized shipments.
What is the free swell volume of your sodium bentonite?
Our natural untreated sodium bentonite achieves a free swell volume of 28 to 35 ml/2g, making it ideal for civil diaphragm walls, piling, and environmental landfill liners.
