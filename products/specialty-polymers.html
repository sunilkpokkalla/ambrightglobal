Technical Mill Test Certificates & Lot Traceability

Every container shipped includes manufacturer Mill Test Certificates (MTC) detailing lot number, melt flow index (ASTM D1238), density (ASTM D1505), tensile strength at yield (ASTM D638), and environmental stress crack resistance (ESCR ASTM D1693). REACH compliant substance declarations and EU/FDA food contact compliance letters are provided on request.

Frequently Asked Questions & Commercial Guidance

Institutional buyer inquiries regarding specifications, maritime shipping, minimum order quantities, and payment terms.

Are these virgin prime polymer granules or recycled?
We export strictly 100% Prime Virgin material with original manufacturer quality certificates. All bags are factory-sealed in 25 kg bags with lot numbers and production dates. Off-grade or reprocessed resins are not shipped unless specifically requested.
What is the container loading capacity for 25 kg bags?
A standard 20' FCL accommodates 18 to 19.5 MT (without pallets) or 16 MT (palletized). A 40' HC accommodates up to 24.75 to 26 MT subject to road weight limits at the destination port.
Do your polymer products comply with European REACH regulations?
Yes. Our polyolefins and specialty chemical compounds are fully pre-registered and compliant with EU REACH Regulation (EC) No 1907/2006. REACH certificates of compliance are issued per shipment.
Can we request specific Melt Flow Index (MFI) adjustments?
Yes. For recurring industrial contracts exceeding 100 MT/month, we can allocate specific reactor runs matching your target MFI and comonomer distribution.
