Frequently Asked Questions & Commercial Guidance

Institutional buyer inquiries regarding specifications, maritime shipping, minimum order quantities, and payment terms.

Do your engineering products include EN 10204 3.1 inspection certificates?
Yes. All critical fasteners, valves, and forged components are shipped with EN 10204 Type 3.1 inspection certificates detailing chemical composition, heat number, yield strength, tensile strength, hardness, and Charpy impact values.
Can we commission third-party inspection (TPI) prior to shipment?
Absolutely. We routinely host Lloyd's Register, TUV, DNV, SGS, and Bureau Veritas inspectors at our manufacturing units and stuffing points for witness testing and dimension verification.
What is the packaging standard for maritime shipment?
Fasteners are packed in 25 kg export cartons, stacked on heat-treated Euro/ISPM-15 wooden pallets, shrink-wrapped, and steel-strapped. Valves are securely crated in plywood boxes with desiccant packs.
